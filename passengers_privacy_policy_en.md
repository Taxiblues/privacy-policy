**2. Scope**

This Privacy Policy describes the processing of personal data relating to users of the Passengers App (the "User" or "Data Subject"), available for download from digital stores such as the Google Play Store and Apple App Store.

**3. Purpose of the App**

Passengers is a platform dedicated to the motorcycle community, designed to facilitate connections between:

- bikers who wish to publish proposals for motorcycle rides, rallies, or outings;
- motorcycle enthusiasts who do not ride but wish to participate as passengers;
- passengers who wish to propose rides to the biker community.

The App allows users to:

- publish proposals for motorcycle rides;
- receive notifications concerning proposals and bookings;
- apply or put themselves forward for a ride;
- evaluate other users on the basis of feedback, experience, motorcycle type, body build, or other factors relevant to participation in rides;
- interact through internal messaging features.

- The App is not a taxi service.
- It does not guarantee users' conduct.

**4. Personal Data Collected**

Based on the information provided by the Data Controller, the App may process the following categories of personal data:

**Data collected directly**

- **Email:** collected for registration, authentication, and account management;
- **Profile photo:** voluntarily provided by the user to personalise their profile;
- **Messages/chat:** content exchanged between users through the App's internal features.

**Data relating to content entered by users**

The App may also process content voluntarily entered by the user, such as:

- ride proposals;
- text descriptions;
- reviews or feedback;
- preferences expressed in the profile;
- any other content uploaded or published within the platform.

**Location data**

To enable the management of departure and meeting points for motorcycle rides, the App may process data relating to the User's geographical location (for example, GPS coordinates or manually entered addresses), strictly to the extent necessary to provide this feature.

**Special categories of data**

The User may voluntarily provide "special categories of data" in their profile or in the privacy sections of digital stores. Such data, which may reveal health-related information, is processed exclusively on the basis of the User's explicit consent and solely to facilitate matching between bikers and passengers.

**6. Purposes of Processing**

Personal data is processed for the following purposes:

- enabling user registration and authentication;
- allowing the creation and management of personal profiles;
- publishing, displaying, and managing proposals for motorcycle rides and events;
- enabling interaction between bikers and passengers;
- managing notifications within the App;
- facilitating mutual selection between users based on factors such as feedback, experience, body build, or motorcycle type;
- ensuring the correct technical operation of the platform;
- complying with legal obligations or requests from competent authorities;
- displaying promotional content or advertising banners, where enabled.

**7. Legal Basis for Processing**

The processing of personal data is based on the following legal grounds, depending on the specific purposes pursued:

a) **Performance of a contract and pre-contractual measures** (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR): for all purposes strictly connected with providing the App's services, such as registration, account management, publication of proposals, interaction between users, and the technical operation of the platform.

b) **User consent** (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR):

- for the installation of cookies or other non-technical tracking tools and for sending marketing communications;
- explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a) GDPR) is also required for processing special categories of data, such as information concerning body build, which the user chooses to provide voluntarily.

c) **Compliance with legal obligations** (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR): to comply with requests from competent authorities or applicable laws.

d) **Legitimate interests of the Data Controller** (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR): for security, fraud prevention, and technical maintenance of the App, provided that these interests are not overridden by the interests or fundamental rights and freedoms of the Data Subject.
